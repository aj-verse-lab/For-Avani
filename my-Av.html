Mumbai can be overwhelming.

Too many voices.
Too many expectations.
Too many things asking for your energy.

And you keep giving.
Even when you are tired.
Even when you are drained.

You don’t have to be strong right now.

Close your eyes for a second.

Imagine this instead.

It’s quiet.
The city feels far away.
You’re sitting next to me.
Your head resting on my shoulder.
My hand gently holding yours.

No rush.
No noise.
Just us.

You are safe here.
AVANI
